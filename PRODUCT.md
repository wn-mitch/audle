# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People age 9 and up, from curious first-time beat makers to experienced electronic-music creators, making a short daily session on a phone or desktop. They need an immediate, musical result without needing production knowledge, while retaining precise controls when they want them.

## Product Purpose

Audle makes, shares, hears, and lets people choose a favorite constrained daily loop. Everyone receives the same eight-source prompt. Play opens on eight sound objects: the first tap starts a loop, later taps bring parts in or out on the next bar, and the selected sound offers six pattern feels plus optional tuning. Arrange exposes the precision timeline, independent cloned voices, and Jev (TypeSafe's choice model), which fills empty tracks from a vetted pattern catalog steered by an optional vibe. Finish keeps the editable loop separate from a bar-aligned live take of mute and solo changes; either result can be replayed, shared by link, or downloaded as WAV. A share link is a short `/s/…` URL held by the share service for 90 days; if that service is unavailable, sharing falls back to the self-contained `#audle=…` link the loop always carries, which never expires. Within five minutes, a new maker should be able to create a layered, recognizably authored result …

## Brand Personality

Buoyant, tactile, clever. The instrument rewards curiosity with sound and visible change, treats people as capable, and remains credible for serious music-making.

## Anti-references

- Full desktop DAW complexity
- Neon cyberpunk club UI
- Patronizing children's software
- Generic card dashboard
- Retro handheld, toy-shadow, or cream-plastic interfaces

## Design Principles

1. Sound on the first gesture.
2. Teach by remixing, never by blocking creation.
3. Every tap should reward with an audible or visible result.
4. Start shallow, reveal optional depth.
5. Constraints create comparability, while cloned voices preserve expression.
6. Touch and keyboard are peers.
7. A shareable personal result belongs within five minutes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support reduced motion, non-color cues, complete keyboard operation, and no gesture-only command. Primary touch targets are at least 48 by 48 CSS pixels; compact controls are at least 44 by 44. Audio requires an explicit user gesture, and shared links fail safely with clear recovery language.
